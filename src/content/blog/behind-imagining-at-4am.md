Counting the breaths beside you and thinking about the day they'll stop. Illness, accidents, a change of heart — a 4 AM afraid of things that haven't happened. The better now is, the more the future frightens.

## The scene it came from

Which of us dies first; the one left behind is probably me — the kind of imagining you only get better at. And then: searching for fingertips under the blanket, confirming there's warmth, and only then getting sleepy. There are nights that can't end without checking.

## Sound notes

88 BPM, E minor. One cold bell tone returns at even intervals through the whole track — like the 4 AM imaginings themselves. The coldest song on the record. It pairs with the 3rd album's track 5 (Worst Case at 3AM), but this time the worst case isn't about who leaves — it's about who is left.

## One line

> 終わりがあるから 今夜が 濃くなるのかな — Maybe it's because it ends that tonight runs this deep

Where the rehearsal of loss runs out, the song answers: knowing it isn't forever, and staying beside them anyway.
