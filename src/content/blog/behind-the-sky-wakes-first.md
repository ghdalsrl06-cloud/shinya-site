The record opens up exactly once, and the reason is outside the window.

## The scene it came from

Only the lower part of the window has changed colour: 「黒じゃ なくなってる / 青に 近い何か」. Not bright yet, but no longer black.

「起こしてないのに / 勝手に 起きる / 待ってないのに / 勝手に 来る」 — **ending the night was never her decision.** The chorus closes it with 「置いていかれる」: left behind.

A bird starts up, a sound that must be there every morning and that she is hearing for the first time: 「起きてる人だけが / 知ってる音が ある / 知らなくても / 生きていける音」. **Knowledge worth nothing.**

And the ending repeats track one: 「見なければ / まだ 夜だった」. In track one it was the clock she shouldn't have looked at. Here it is the window.

## Sound notes

**The only track that opens upward.** Bright bell synths in the high register, an airy pad, the low end taken out. **F major — the only major key on the album.** The sky brightens first, so the sound does too.

## One line

> 起こしてないのに / 勝手に 起きる — nobody woke it / it wakes on its own

The brightest track on a record about not sleeping is the one where the choice is taken away.
