The second SHINYA CLUB record opens by not moving. On an album built entirely on one 128 BPM Jersey club pattern, the first track is the only one where that kick never arrives.

## The scene it came from

Still dressed, lying on top of the covers, counting the grain in the ceiling and losing track at the fourth line. The charger is too far away and the phone is at four percent. The line the whole album grows out of is here: 「일어나야 하는 건 잘 알고 있다 / 몸만이 대답을 안 한다」 — it isn't a willpower problem. The head has already agreed. The body just doesn't answer.

Where 「Paper Luck」 sent its narrator out into the street to bet a day on a pickle jar, this one starts flat on its back with nothing in its pockets.

## Sound notes

128 BPM in name only — no five-kick bounce anywhere. One low pulse beats like a slow heart, breath left audible in the take, and the stutter snare exists only as a far-off echo. A minor. A song about not being able to move gets a rhythm that can't move either.

## One line

> それが 楽で / それが こわい — that it's easy / is what scares me

Nobody would notice if morning came like this. The relief and the fear arrive in the same breath, and then the record spends six tracks moving anyway.
