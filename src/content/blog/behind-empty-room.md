The album opens on the night everything has already been carried out.

## The scene it came from

Three boxes hold everything: 「思ってたより / ずっと 少ない」. With the furniture gone the footsteps come back off the walls — 「こんな音 したっけ」 — and the room turns unfamiliar without changing at all.

The chorus sets the premise the whole album runs on: 「声が 返ってくる / 返ってくるだけ / **広い わけじゃない / 何も ないだけ**」. It is not spaciousness. It is absence.

Only the wall keeps a record — the patches where furniture stood are a shade off, and 「そこに 何が あったか / **私しか 知らない**」. She does not mourn it. Whoever arrives tomorrow will read the room as new, and 「それで いいと 思う / それが いいと 思う」.

## Sound notes

**No drums at all** — one of only two tracks like that, with the closer. A single Rhodes chord spreads into a long room reverb, footsteps and breath left in, and the sub-bass enters only on the chorus. **The most reverberant track on the album.**

## One line

> 声が 返ってくる / 返ってくるだけ / 広い わけじゃない / 何も ないだけ — my voice comes back / it only comes back / it isn’t that it’s wide / there’s just nothing in it

The second line of the album is 「歩くと 響く」. Keep it in mind — the last line of the album answers it.
