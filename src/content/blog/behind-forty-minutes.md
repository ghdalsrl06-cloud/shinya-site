The record does not talk itself out of the floor. It stands up and walks.

## The scene it came from

There is no plan: 「座ってられない 靴を 履く」. The convenience store is right there, and she goes 「わざと 遠い方」 — deliberately the far way, forty minutes of it.

The reason is physical, not emotional: 「汗が 出るまで 歩けば / 考えたくない ことが / 足の裏に 落ちていく」. **Things you do not want to think about, falling out through the soles of your feet.** The city cooperates in the small way empty cities do — 「信号が 全部 青に なる」.

Then the tell: she reaches the store, and 「何も 買わずに 出てきた」. It was never the destination. And the album's rule comes back through a different limb: track two was 「手は もう 聞いてない」, and here it is 「足が 覚えてる / 道は 考えない / 体が 先に 行く」.

She takes the long way home too, then goes round once more.

## Sound notes

**The steadiest groove on the record** — an unbroken walking pulse with one figure that does not change from the first bar to the last. Track four stopped the sound dead; this is where it comes back, as footsteps rather than as music.

## One line

> 体が 先に 行く — my body goes first

Hands in track two, feet here, and in the last line of track seven the hand again. The thinking always arrives second.
