This is the centre of the record, and it answers the previous album directly.

## The scene it came from

The previous record spent a whole album inside **her own** four o'clock, opening with 「午前4時になった」. Here that hour is already gone: 「私の四時は / もう 過ぎた / 過ぎて しまえば / 何でも なかった」. **The line just before that album's final chorus was 「四時は 過ぎた / 過ぎてしまえば / 何でも なかった」** — the same words, walked somewhere else.

Where they go is outward: 「でも 今も / どこかは 四時で / 誰かが 同じ / 顔を してる」. **Her four o'clock becomes a stranger's.**

Line the clocks up and they all disagree and **all of them are right** — 「全部 違う / どれも 正しい / どれも 今だ」. The belief the album breaks is 「四時が 一つだと / 思ってた」.

And the ending turns the loneliest idea inside out: 「私だけだと / 思ってた夜が / 世界の どこかで / 今 始まってる」.

## Sound notes

**The slowest and widest track here**, at 68 BPM. The chords are voiced far apart over a long low drone, and **several clock-like ticks are layered slightly out of phase with each other** — there being more than one clock is the whole point of the song.

## One line

> 私だけだと / 思ってた夜が / 世界の どこかで / 今 始まってる — the night I thought / was only mine / is starting now / somewhere in the world

The last record made four o’clock a private hour. This one gives it away.
