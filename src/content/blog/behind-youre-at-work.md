The read receipt does not appear, and for once that is fine.

## The scene it came from

「既読が つかない / つかなくて いい」. It is the middle of their working day.

What changed is what she counts: 「昔なら / 数えてた / 今は 時計を / 見るだけ」. Minutes became time zones.

And the album's conclusion is here, three tracks early: 「返事が 遅いのは / 嫌われたからじゃない / 太陽の 位置が / 違うだけ」. **Not a problem of feeling — a problem of astronomy.** So she refuses the word for it: 「すれ違いを / すれ違いと 呼ばない」.

Messages land in hours neither of them is awake for, and land anyway: 「それでも 届く / それが いい」. Then the quietest shift on the record: 「待つのを やめたら / 待てるように なった / 来ないんじゃない / まだ 昼なんだ」.

## Sound notes

**The fastest and busiest track here**, at 78 BPM: tight sixteenth hi-hats, a bass cut short, an arrangement crowded as though something else were going on. The sound says the other side is at work.

## One line

> 返事が 遅いのは / 嫌われたからじゃない / 太陽の 位置が / 違うだけ — a reply being slow / isn’t being disliked / it’s the sun / sitting somewhere else

Four albums of reading silence as rejection, and here it is finally read as geometry.
