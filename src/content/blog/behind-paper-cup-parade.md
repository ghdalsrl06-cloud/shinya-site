The closer collects the whole album and walks it out the door: the coin from the drain, a dented crown, a sky bottled up. No duet this time, no dice, no ticket — just one person, everything they've picked up, and a road. A parade doesn't need a crowd. It needs a decision.

## The scene it came from

Spilled plans on the floor, blue ink on a thumb, an old dog watching from the shade. Where the main line albums end by looping back to midnight, the CLUB line's first record ends by simply walking forward — map wrong, clock stopped, going anyway.

## Sound notes

128 BPM and the shortest, straightest track on the album: no breakdown, no fake ending, the bounce just marches. Every found-sound from the previous six tracks makes a one-bar cameo in the outro — the squeak, the coins, the crayon tin, the scratch — like the parade passing each song on its way out.

## One line

> 地図が 嘘でも それでも 行く — even if the map is lying, I'm going anyway

The main line asks how to survive the night. The CLUB line answers by walking through it, carrying all its paper luck.
