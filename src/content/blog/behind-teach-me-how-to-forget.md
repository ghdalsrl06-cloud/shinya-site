Someone who thought no one would know as long as they smiled by day and only broke at night — stopped mid-street by a similar silhouette. Unable to stand in a supermarket aisle because a song they used to love came on. This is the moment the lie about being stronger stops.

## The scene it came from

They taught us how to memorize, how to keep, how to accumulate — nobody ever taught how to erase. That's why the title isn't a command but a plea: teach me how to forget.

## Sound notes

96 BPM, C-sharp minor. The climax of the 2nd album. The bass runs saturated, pushed to the edge of distortion, and this is the only track on the record where the voice breaks open. Five songs of holding it in collapse at once — so the sound had to collapse with it.

## One line

> 残し方しか 習わなかった — All I was ever taught was how to keep

After this song, only dawn light remains on the album. In the next track — after the collapse — a major key opens for the first time.
