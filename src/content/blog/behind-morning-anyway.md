The last track doesn't close the album. It leaves the door open on purpose.

## The scene it came from

The edge of the sky goes white while he isn't looking. He hasn't slept at all and he's fine anyway. Birds start up — one, then two — and he realizes he heard today begin before anyone else did.

What he refuses to do is defend the night. He doesn't claim it was worth it. He just declines to call it wasted: 「잠들지 않은 밤을 / 헛됐다고는 / 오늘은 말하지 않기로 한다 / 그것만으로 된다」. That restraint is the most honest thing on the record.

Then the detail that makes the whole line worth continuing: 「안 자고 맞은 아침은 / 조금 색이 다르다 / 그걸 아는 사람이 / 이 도시에 몇 명은 있다」. Not a crowd. A few.

## Sound notes

Chords that stayed closed all album finally open. Plucks and Rhodes layered wide, with birdsong placed far back in the mix. The kick softens but never stops — the night ends and the rhythm stays.

## One line

> また 来る夜に / 同じことを する — the next night that comes / I'll do the same thing

Where 「Paper Luck」 walked out into a one-person parade, this one admits the night is not a single event but a repeating one. Which is also a promise about the third CLUB record.
