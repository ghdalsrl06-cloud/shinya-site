Six tracks in minor keys, and then this one opens. It's the only major key on the record, and it's the track where everything is lost.

## The scene it came from

A single line of light through the gap in the curtain, falling to the floor and running straight. She could step out of it and can't: 「이미 와 버렸다 / **이미 끝나 버렸다**」.

The pre-chorus names what actually hurts: 「눈부신 건 / 빛이 아니라 / 이걸로 끝이라고 / **알아 버리는 것**」. It isn't the brightness. It's the knowing.

And now, finally, she can see his face — after a whole night of only a voice. But she won't say it was better: 「보이는 편이 / 나았던 건지 / 지금도 모르겠다 / 아마 계속」.

Only the last chorus grows a tail: 「終わることも / きれいだった」 — *even the ending was beautiful.* **The only track on the album that ends open.**

## Sound notes

**The only major key on the record.** The Rhodes opens wide, the 808 turns warm and round, and a single bright guitar line arrives late. The other six tracks are all minor.

## One line

> 暗いままなら 続けられた / でも 続けたい だけじゃ ない — if it had stayed dark we could have continued / but wanting to continue is not all of it

The most adult sentence in the SHINYA catalogue. Wanting it isn't the only thing that's true.
