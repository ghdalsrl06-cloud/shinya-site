Every record that ends in **I would do it again** has to earn it. This is where it gets paid for.

## The scene it came from

The hook is two words — 「Can't undo」 — and the room goes with it: 「急に 静かになった / 音楽も 自分で 止めた」. **She turned her own music off.** On an album at 100 BPM, that is the loudest thing that happens.

She sits on the floor and holds her knees, and cannot place the person from twenty minutes ago: 「さっきの 私は 誰だったんだろう」. What survives is very short: 「取り消せないし やり直せない / 本当のことは それだけ / あとは 全部 言い訳」.

Time is exact and unhelpful — 「時計が 四時を 指してる / 朝まで 三時間 / 長い」.

And then the two most honest lines on the album: 「怖いのは 後悔じゃない / 明日の 私が これを 引き受けること」. **What frightens her is not regret. It is that tomorrow's self has to carry it.** Track one filed that person away as a stranger; here she becomes a real person who will have to pick this up.

The last line refuses the excuse it has just been handed: 「わかってて やった」.

## Sound notes

**The sound stops.** One kick per bar, a single pad, and room tone — the sound of a room with the music switched off, used as the track. Everything else on the record moves; this one sits still on purpose.

## One line

> 怖いのは 後悔じゃない / 明日の 私が これを 引き受けること — what scares me is not regret / it is tomorrow's me having to carry this

Three tracks later she will say 「明日の私が なんとかする」 and mean it kindly. She could not have got there without being afraid of her first.
