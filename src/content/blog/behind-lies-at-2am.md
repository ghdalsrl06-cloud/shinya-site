If only one song from this album could survive, it would be this one. 午前二時の嘘 is the heart of the project — the blog's name, "Music for 2AM," comes from here.

## The scene it came from

A street after the last train has faded. Neon bleeding into rain-wet asphalt, and under a streetlight, people saying things they would never say at noon. The whole track started from one question: are the things we say at 2AM the lies — or are the things we say in daylight?

## Sound notes

The intro sits on a real rain texture, with a synth flickering over it like a failing streetlight. The chorus is the loudest moment on the album, and it lands exactly on "嘘でもいいから" — even a lie will do. The loudest voice on the record is begging to be lied to. That irony is the design of the song.

## One line

> 嘘でもいいから 「愛してる」って言って！ — Even a lie will do, so say "I love you"!

The only lyric on the album with an exclamation mark. I wanted her to shout exactly once — here.
