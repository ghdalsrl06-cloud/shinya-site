The blurriest track on the record, and the one where the coat first appears.

## The scene it came from

Not asleep, not awake. The other person's voice rings inside the dream, and she can't tell which side she's on. 「깨우지 마 / 아직 반은 / 저쪽에」.

**The coat from track five shows up here first** — she's sleeping under it. 「코트 냄새가 / 베개에 옮는다 / 그걸로 충분해 / 그걸로 잠들 수 있어」. And she knows exactly what it's worth: 「아침에는 사라진다 / 알고 있지만」.

The bridge is the whole song's thesis: 「반은 깨어 있어 / 반은 없어 / 그 정도가 / 딱 좋아」. Being fully present would mean facing it. Half is survivable.

## Sound notes

The lead vocal is layered and **one of those layers is played backwards.** The whole arrangement is blurred with a low-pass, and the snaps are pushed down to brushes. The least defined track on the album, deliberately.

## One line

> どっちも 同じ / どっちも あなた — both are the same / both are you

Dream or room, it doesn't matter — the answer is the same person either way. The album's most tender line, delivered half-conscious.
