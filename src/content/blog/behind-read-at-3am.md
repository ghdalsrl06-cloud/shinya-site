2:48 AM. One line, revised for twelve minutes: "How are you?" Sending just that took half a year. Then two characters attach — Read — and after that, nothing moves.

## The scene it came from

Knowing that not answering is also an answer, and still getting through tonight on the single fact of having been read. Time stands still in this song: 3 AM, inside the screen, only one person waiting.

## Sound notes

84 BPM, E minor. The stillest track on the record. A single notification-like tone rings at even intervals all the way through — sound keeping the seat where the reply should be. The silence between those tones is the loudest thing in the song.

## One line

> Still there, still there, 読んだんだね それだけで 今日は 眠れる — You read it — and just for that, tonight I can sleep

A sad kind of relief. Nothing came back, and yet there are nights when being read is enough to fall asleep on.
