A screen lighting up at 1 AM, a name glowing on it — that's the entire song. "Still awake?" Five characters that should bring joy, and hands that shake instead. This is a song about the night when happiness and dread arrive with the same notification sound.

## The scene it came from

Waiting ten seconds before letting the read receipt show. Carrying the charger around the room, afraid something ends if the battery dies. Every detail here draws one state: being scared of getting close to happy. Once you've known the nights when no notification comes, the nights when one does come turn frightening too.

## Sound notes

98 BPM, F minor. The only track on the record with a bright synth. The chime that repeats behind the chorus is designed like an actual notification tone — the same sound looping while the chords shift under it, so it reads as joy in one bar and anxiety in the next.

## One line

> 通知が来ない夜を もう知ってしまってるから — Because I already know the nights when no notification comes

The trembling in this song isn't butterflies — it's learned fear. Which is why the brightest synth on the album was meant to sound like the saddest one.
