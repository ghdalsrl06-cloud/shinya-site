A different person, but the fingers choose the same road. This is the song for the moment when the way you typed a deleted name comes back for the new one — the realization that only the other person changed, and you haven't changed at all.

## The scene it came from

Measuring where the read mark sits, counting the length of replies, preparing to lose before even falling. The person who stood in front of the convenience store in the 2nd album's 癖のように is standing in the same spot months later — with someone new. Experience protected nothing.

## Sound notes

90 BPM, D minor. The same arpeggio keeps returning through the whole track — and the device is lifted directly from 癖のように, track 2 of the 2nd album. That's not an accident; it's the design. A habit repeating across albums is itself the message of this song.

## One line

> 変わったのは 相手だけで 私は 何も 変わってない — The only thing that changed is who; I haven't changed at all

If you've followed the series from the start, you'll recognize the arpeggio the moment it returns. That déjà vu is the most important instrument in the track.
