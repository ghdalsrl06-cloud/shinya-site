Two years of looking up at the same ceiling, and this is the first time she sees it.

## The scene it came from

The bed went out yesterday. A blanket on bare floor, and it is hard.

「天井が 遠い / こんなに 高かった / 毎日 見てたのに / 初めて 見る」. Nothing about the ceiling changed; the thing she used to look at it from is gone.

The most exact description of the night before a move sits in the second verse: 「何も できないのに / なぜか 疲れてる / やることは 終わって / 時間だけ 余る / **余った時間が / いちばん 長い**」. The work being finished is what makes the hours long.

The bridge quietly sets up the closer: 「この床で / 最初の夜も 寝た / 何も なかった / 今と 同じ」. She slept on this floor the first night too. The room she is leaving is the room she arrived in.

## Sound notes

**The lowest sub bass on the album.** The harmony opens wide and the percussion thins to a single soft brush. Everything sits under the vocal — she is on the floor, so the ceiling is far.

## One line

> 余った時間が / いちばん 長い — and the time left over / is the longest

This is the middle of the record and the middle of the night. From here the album starts closing things down, one at a time.
