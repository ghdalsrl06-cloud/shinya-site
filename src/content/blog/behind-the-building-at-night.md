The building does not know she is leaving, and carries on exactly as usual.

## The scene it came from

Footsteps upstairs at eleven exactly, the same as every day. Pipes knock somewhere; someone is running water. 「起きてる人が いる / 名前は 知らない」.

The turn is in the pre-chorus: 「うるさいと 思ってた / ずっと 思ってた / **今夜だけは / 聞いていたい**」. Nothing about the noise changed. Only the number of nights left did.

A hallway light has been out for half a year and nobody reported it. 「明日から / 私も 言えない / 直らないまま / 覚えてる」 — from tomorrow she loses even the standing to complain, so the fault becomes a memory instead.

The closing verse is the coolest moment on the album: 「明日 誰かが / ここに 来る / 上の足音を / **その人が 聞く**」. The building continues without her, and the same footsteps become someone else's.

## Sound notes

**The percussion is made of domestic noise** — knocks, taps, the ring of a pipe. The Rhodes sits quietly underneath. This is a song about sound, so the sounds through the wall are in front of the instruments.

## One line

> 明日 誰かが / ここに 来る / 上の足音を / その人が 聞く — tomorrow someone / will come here / and the footsteps upstairs / will be theirs to hear

No resentment in it, and no sentiment either. The building was never hers.
