This song sits at the exact center of the album, where time stops moving. 夜に溺れる is about sinking into the dark and not particularly wanting to be rescued — that strange calm.

## The scene it came from

Exactly what the cover shows: shallow water on tiled floor, hair spreading out, eyes on the ceiling. Not drowning — submerged. Dangerous but comfortable, needing to leave but not wanting to. The song translates that feeling of night into water.

## Sound notes

Every sound in this track runs through a low-pass filter, like everything is heard from underwater. The sidechain is timed to a breathing cycle, so the whole track swells and sinks like slow lungs. The vocal reverb is the longest on the album — a voice dispersing through water. In the "Come closer" bridge the filter gradually opens, but it isn't surfacing; it's more like sinking deeper while your eyes adjust to the dark.

## One line

> 夜に溺れる 名前さえ忘れるほど — Drowning in the night, deep enough to forget my own name

Forgetting your name means forgetting your daytime self. On this album, that isn't a sad thing.
