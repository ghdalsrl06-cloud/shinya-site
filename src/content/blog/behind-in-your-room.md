The album is titled 「誰かの部屋で」 — *in someone's room* — but the last song is 「あなたの部屋で」 — *in your room*. 誰か becomes あなた. Past six strangers' rooms, the room that remains at the end is **the room of whoever is listening.**

## The scene

The only second-person song on the album. Tracks 1–7 each speak their own night; track 8 speaks to you. "Not even knowing which track this is, you listened all the way here. I don't know your name or your face, but I know that you're awake right now." Then it names the seven rooms one by one and says: every one of them held someone truly awake tonight. So if you can't sleep either — it isn't yours to carry alone.

## Sound notes

72 BPM, D Dorian — **the same pulse as track 1.** The thinnest arrangement on the album, and the outro is a cappella, every instrument gone. The opening signature 「深夜。まだ起きてる。」 is heard through a phone filter only in this song; as the track runs, the filter peels away, and in the last line the same words arrive right beside you. The album closes on the words that opened it.

## One line

> この声は 明日も ここにいる — this voice will be here tomorrow too

This album doesn't console. It only confirms that we're awake at the same hour. "Goodnight" — and then the final line, the same as the first. 深夜。まだ起きてる。
