The album's centerpiece game: instead of deciding anything, roll a crayon and obey. It sounds like giving up control. It's actually the opposite — choosing, on purpose, to let nonsense pick, because nonsense has better taste than anxiety does.

## The scene it came from

A glove box with a blue crayon in it, a dog staring at shoes in the hallway like it knows the answer. The main line albums are full of people paralyzed by choices at 3AM. This track is the CLUB line's reply: stop asking twice. Roll. Go.

## Sound notes

128 BPM with the most aggressive chops on the record — the vocal gets diced into dice, literally cut to single syllables and re-thrown each bar, so the hook never lands the same way twice. A rattling percussion loop (an actual crayon in a tin, shaken) runs under the whole drop.

## One line

> 二度は 聞かない ただ サイコロを振る — I don't ask twice, I just roll the dice

The whole main-line discography asks twice. This is the first song that doesn't.
