An unfamiliar name, an unfamiliar perfume, a well-rehearsed smile. The night that was supposed to be "anyone will do" turns into time spent searching for that one person. The subject is the failure of replacement.

## The scene it came from

Ice ringing in a glass, the music too loud. Standing next to someone new turned out to be this quiet — when standing next to that person was so loud. A song that draws the quietest heart in the noisiest room.

## Sound notes

100 BPM, A minor. The only track on the record with club reverb. The kick was deliberately placed far away, as if heard through a wall — the distance of someone whose body is in the club while their heart stands outside. The coldest, most glamorous sound on the album.

## One line

> 誰でもよかった 夜のはずが 君を探す時間になる — A night when anyone would do becomes time spent looking for you

The glamour here is camouflage. The louder the sound, the sharper the absence.
