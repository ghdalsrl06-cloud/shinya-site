Choosing the opening track didn't take long. Every night begins the same way — with a decision you already know is bad. 毒でいいの turns that exact moment into sound.

## The scene it came from

11PM, elevator numbers falling. Footsteps of someone who left their daytime self in the room. The first lyric — "the elevator numbers falling down" — was really the blueprint for the whole track: the song itself keeps descending, floor by floor.

## Sound notes

A low pulsing bass with a whispered vocal on top. Even the chorus never shouts — bad decisions aren't made loudly, they're made quietly. The synths collapse instead of the voice. For the line about "sweet numbness," the chorus synth is deliberately detuned, just slightly off.

## One line

> 毒でいいの もう一口だけ — Poison is fine, just one more sip

"One more sip" is the attitude of the entire album: knowing you should stop and not stopping. That feeling carries all the way to track 7.
