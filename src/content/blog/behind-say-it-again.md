The center of the album, and the point where the two people are physically closest.

## The scene it came from

She pretended not to hear what he said. She heard all of it. Now she wants it repeated — and the specification is precise: 「한 번만 더 / 같은 목소리로 / **같은 거리에서** / 말해 줬으면 해」. Not the words. The conditions.

The reason arrives in the second verse: 「아침이 오면 / 전부 멀어진다 / 그러니까 지금 중에」. She's not collecting affection, she's collecting evidence — breath landing on skin, fingers touching, each one counted as 「그것도 기억한다」·「그것도 남긴다」.

The bridge knows exactly what it's doing: 「말하지 않아도 / 알고 있지만 / **알고 있으니까 / 듣고 싶어**」.

## Sound notes

The **closest and driest mix on the album** — almost no reverb on the lead. Ad-libs answer in every gap; the `(Say it again)` tails at the end of the chorus are those answers. The Rhodes is reduced to long held chords.

## One line

> 覚えて おきたい / 形の まま — I want to keep it / in the shape it has

Not the memory of it. The shape. She's trying to store something that only exists while it's being said.
