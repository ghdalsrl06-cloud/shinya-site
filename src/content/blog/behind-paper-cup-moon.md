Two tracks of loving objects, and then this: the first CLUB song aimed at an actual person. But it keeps the line's grammar — the love is measured in cold fries, red ink on a hand, a paper cup on the dashboard standing in for the moon.

## The scene it came from

A parked car, food going cold, nobody in a hurry to leave. The moon in this song is a chipped paper cup — about to spill, held up at the last second, and somehow better than the real one. Loving something *because* it's about to spill is the most SHINYA CLUB feeling on the record.

## Sound notes

128 BPM but softer: the bounce is padded with warm chords, and the bed-squeak triplets are swapped for a muted cowbell. The chorus stacks a second vocal a sixth below, so "take me there" lands like two people saying it at slightly different times.

## One line

> こぼれそう こぼれそう それでも 好きだ — it's gonna spill, it's gonna spill — and I love it anyway

Every love in this line is a paper cup. The song just says it out loud.
