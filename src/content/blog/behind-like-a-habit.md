The head knows it's over, but the right hand opens the screen first. The place where the name used to be is empty, yet the fingers remember the route. This is a song about the things the body remembers before the mind does.

## The scene it came from

Typing and deleting until morning comes. They say forgetting isn't a decision — it's noticing one day that you've stopped. That day hasn't come yet, and this song lives inside that "yet."

## Sound notes

88 BPM, D minor. The same arpeggio phrase keeps circling through the entire track. A habit is repetition, so repetition itself became the instrument — a structure that keeps returning to the same spot no matter how it tries to leave, exactly like the lyrics.

## One line

> 癖のように 名前を打って 送れないまま 朝が来る — Typing the name like a habit; morning comes with nothing sent

A night where unsent messages pile up. The arpeggio was made to sound like it keeps spinning somewhere even after the album ends.
