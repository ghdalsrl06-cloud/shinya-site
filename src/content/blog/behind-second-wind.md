This is where the album gets its name, and where the kick finally arrives. Track one held it back for three minutes so that this downbeat would land like a switch being thrown.

## The scene it came from

Four AM air, cold enough to reach the bottom of the lungs. One breath in and the body sits up on its own. The narrator can't explain it: 「어디서 온 걸까 / 이 힘은 / 다 썼을 텐데」. Shoes half on, keys in hand, out the door — and crucially, 「갈 곳은 안 정했다 / 안 정해도 된다」. Having no destination is the point. This isn't motivation. It's a phenomenon.

## Sound notes

The Jersey club kick enters the album here for the first time, with bright synth plucks over the top, a punchy sub, and rimshot snares. D major. Because track one refused the kick, the moment it lands here reads as an event rather than a groove.

## One line

> 二度目の息は 理由を 聞かない — the second wind doesn't ask for reasons

The last verse gives up on explaining. Being tired is still true — 「지친 건 사실이다 / 그래도 움직인다」. The album never resolves that contradiction; it just runs on it.
