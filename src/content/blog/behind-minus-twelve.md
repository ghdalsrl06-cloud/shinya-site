Twelve subtracted, twelve added. That is the whole song.

## The scene it came from

「十二 引けば / そっちの時間 / 足せば 私」. She did not set out to memorise it: 「覚えたくて / 覚えたんじゃない / 何度も やったら / 体が 覚えた」. **It was repetition, not effort.** At first she counted on her fingers; now she knows on sight.

And the cost of getting good at it is the point: 「それだけのこと / なのに 遠い / 数字が 遠い」. **Being fluent in the distance does not shorten it.**

The last verse looks for a fair hour and finds only a bad one: 「真ん中は どこ / 六時間ずつ / どっちも 無理を / してる時間」.

## Sound notes

**Finger snaps are the rhythm** — short and regular, the sound of counting. The sub-bass is clipped short and one Rhodes figure repeats in place. A song about counting, arranged so it counts.

## One line

> それだけのこと / なのに 遠い / 数字が 遠い — that’s all it is / and still it’s far / the number is far

The shortest track here, and the one that does the least. That is deliberate — it is a sum, not a scene.
