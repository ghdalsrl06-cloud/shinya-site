There was one rule for writing the breakup song: don't make it sad — make it beautiful. 最後のキスは嘘 is the result. A song where beauty itself becomes the cruelty.

## The scene it came from

The red in the cover is hallway light leaking under a door — the color of the moment just before it closes. One person saying "let's end this" with far too gentle a face; the other realizing, precisely because of that gentleness, that the words are a lie. A key on the table, a returned ring — a meeting arranged to end things that refuses to end.

## Sound notes

The chorus melody is the prettiest thing on the album, written that way on purpose. Underneath it sits the heaviest, most distorted 808 on the record. Glass on top, collapse below — the structure of "a gentle-faced goodbye," translated directly into sound. Right before the last chorus, everything drops out and only "行かないで!" — don't go! — remains. That silence is the real climax.

## One line

> 嘘だって言ってよ — Tell me it was a lie

On track 3 she begged to be lied to. Here, she begs to be told that the goodbye she just heard was the lie. The same plea, returned from the opposite direction.
