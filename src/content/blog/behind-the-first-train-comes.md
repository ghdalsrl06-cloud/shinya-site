The gap in the curtains turning white, watched with eyes that never slept. Rails crying in the distance — a sound they'd been waiting for without knowing it. Nothing is resolved, but the night ends.

## The scene it came from

The realization that the border between yesterday and today never actually existed. This album isn't a story of getting over someone — it's the story of one night of learning you can walk without getting over them. The answer lives in this track: you can go on living without having erased them.

## Sound notes

90 BPM, A **major** — the only major key in all seven tracks. The reverb that covered the whole album lifts here, and dawn light enters the sound for the first time. A low rumble shaped like a first train carries the song out.

## One line

> 始発が来る 何も解決しないまま それでも 朝は来る — The first train comes with nothing resolved; even so, morning comes

"I made it to the first train alone" — that's the sentence the 2nd album closes on. Not a great victory: just the record of someone who walked through one night.
