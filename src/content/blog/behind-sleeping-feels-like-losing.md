Not having a reason turns out to be the reason.

## The scene it came from

She searches and comes up empty: 「仕事でも ないし / 待ってる人も いない / それでも 起きてる」. Nothing is keeping her up, and she stays up.

The title stands in where the reason should be: 「寝たら負けみたいで / 負けたことに なる / 誰と 戦ってるのか / それも 分からない」. **A contest with no opponent** — so it can't be won either: 「勝つ日も ない / 負ける日も ない / ただ 起きてる」.

And then the plainest admission on the record: 「やめ方が 分からない」 — she doesn't know how to stop.

## Sound notes

**The first kick on the album**, halftime. A round sub-bass and one muted guitar note answering into each gap. E minor, and the tempo steps up to 88.

## One line

> 誰と 戦ってるのか / それも 分からない — who I’m fighting / I don’t know that either

Keep the word 「負け」. The closing track turns it over.
