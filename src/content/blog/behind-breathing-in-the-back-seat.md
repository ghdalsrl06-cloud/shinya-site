The 4th album closed on a first morning together. The 5th opens a completely different door — for the first time, SHINYA sings **someone else's night**, and the first room is a taxi's back seat. A passenger gives the address, goes quiet, and falls asleep in the rear-view mirror.

## The scene

Asking nothing is this person's job; saying nothing is their kindness. All day they ask strangers where they're going, and decide their own way home last. When they were young they just wanted the shift to end — now the five minutes before waking a passenger are the quietest of the day.

## Sound notes

72 BPM, D Dorian. Brushed drums and a tape-saturated Rhodes form the skeleton. Listen closely and a turn-signal click is folded into the rhythm — the song's metronome was inside the car all along.

## One line

> 今は この五分が いちばん静かだ — now these five minutes are the quietest I have

Every song on this album opens with the same line — 「深夜。まだ起きてる。」(Midnight. Still awake.) The room changes; the line doesn't. Eight rooms, one voice. The series starts here.
