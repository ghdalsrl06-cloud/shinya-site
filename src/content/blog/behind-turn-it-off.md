The album closes by taking away the one thing it opened with.

## The scene it came from

The key goes in the mailbox. One more look at the room: 「何も 忘れてない / 確認は した / もう 戻らない / 戻る理由が ない」.

Then the line the whole record was walking toward: 「ずっと 消せなかった / 何年も ずっと / **今夜は / 消せる**」. And the chorus refuses to make it a triumph — 「Turn it off / それだけ / 何年も かかった / **それだけのことに**」. Years, for something that small.

With the light off the room becomes the room she moved into: 「暗くなった部屋は / 入った日と 同じ / 何も なくて / 広くて / ここから 始めて / ここで 終わる / **それで いい / それが いい**」. Track one's 「それで いいと 思う / それが いいと 思う」 comes back shortened. **What was self-reassurance at the start of the night is a verdict by the end of it.**

And the last line closes the album: 「ドアを 閉める / 音が しない / **響く部屋が / もう ない**」.

## Sound notes

**The drums drop away and the track returns to the position track one held** — Rhodes and sub-bass only. And at the very end **the reverb tail is cut rather than faded.** The room that rang loudest on track one loses its sound entirely here.

## One line

> ドアを 閉める / 音が しない / 響く部屋が / もう ない — I close the door / it makes no sound / there’s no room left / to echo in

「響」 appears exactly twice on this album: 「歩くと 響く」 in the second line of track one, and 「響く部屋が / もう ない」 in the last line of track seven. That is why the tail is cut instead of faded — the light is off.

The previous album had 「Don't Turn the Light On」 at track two. This one ends on 「Turn It Off」. Two records in the AFTER line, the light named twice, pointing opposite ways.
