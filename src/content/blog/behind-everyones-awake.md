In the seventh room the album unties. Four lit windows across the street — the same four, every night without fail. This is the song of someone who breathes easier just knowing those people didn't sleep tonight either.

## The scene

Look closely at the view from this window and the first six songs are all in it. A taxi passes with someone in the back seat (track 1), the konbini light stays on (track 2), a train sounds in the distance (track 5), last train or first train — that first train is the 2nd album's — and behind the lit windows are a hospital room, an emptied room, a kitchen. **I thought my night alone was special — this was just an ordinary 3AM.** The whole album's release lives in that one line.

## Sound notes

88 BPM, E minor — the widest song on the album. It starts at a whisper and opens completely; in the final chorus three voices stack and, for the first time, melisma appears. The single voice that sang eight rooms becomes, here, several.

## One line

> これは ただの 午前三時だった — this was just an ordinary 3AM

The windows across go dark one by one, and the outro closes on "goodnight, unknown someone, somewhere out there" — a greeting the next song picks up.
