They fell asleep first — going quiet mid-sentence. You pull the blanket back over them and stay awake, watching. For the first time in this series, the reason for not sleeping isn't anxiety. It's wanting to keep looking.

## The scene it came from

The shadow of lashes falling on a cheek; shoulders rising and falling with each breath. A night where the sound of living, right there beside you, is almost enough to make you cry. And the song's core sentence — losing is frightening because now you have something.

## Sound notes

80 BPM, G minor. The slowest track on the record — and there are **no drums at all**. Only room tone and long pads remain. It's a song about holding your breath so someone won't wake; a beat would have broken the spell. Even a heartbeat feels too loud on this night.

## One line

> 失くすのが怖いのは 持ってしまったから — Losing you scares me because now I have you

Albums 1–3 feared beginnings; the 4th fears loss. The pivot of the whole record lives in this line.
