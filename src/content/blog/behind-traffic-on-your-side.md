The other side is noisy, and that turns out to be the good news.

## The scene it came from

Cars, one horn, someone laughing. On her side: 「こっちは 何も / 鳴っていない / 冷蔵庫の音 / それだけ」.

**The noise is what she is glad of** — 「そっちが うるさい / それが うれしい / 生きてる音が / 聞こえてくる」. Traffic as proof of life.

Then the two time zones split inside one call: 「同じ通話の / 中に あるのに / 片方は 昼で / 片方は 夜だ」. And the reason she won't hang up is not the person but **the sound**: 「切りたくない / 音が 消えるから / そっちの昼が / 消えるから」.

## Sound notes

**The only track that lets outside sound in.** Muffled traffic and voices sit low in one channel while the near side is kept dry and close. The vocal is quiet, as though listening to something else while singing, and the sentences do not finish.

## One line

> そっちが うるさい / それが うれしい — your side is loud / and I’m glad of it

Everything else on this record is quiet. This is the one place the other side is actually audible, and it is the one place the distance feels small.
