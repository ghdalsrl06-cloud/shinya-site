Track one held its breath. Here the low end arrives, and with it the album's first real refusal.

## The scene it came from

A hand reaches for the light switch and stops halfway. She doesn't even want to hear the click. The reasoning inverts what you'd expect: 「안 보이는 편이 / 오히려 잘 보여 / 얼굴이라든가 시간이라든가 / 전부 필요 없어」.

The dark here is **a choice, not an escape.** Turning the light on wouldn't reveal the truth — it would reveal the ending: 「밝게 하면 / 끝나는 게 보인다」.

The bridge admits she tried once. 「한 번만 / 켜려고 했다 / 손가락이 기억한다 / 그 망설임을」 — the hand remembers what the mind won't say.

## Sound notes

The **808 sub-bass enters the album for the first time here**, catching the silence track one left behind. Finger snaps on the back beat, muted guitar answering the empty spaces. Half-time throughout.

## One line

> 明るく したら / 終わるのが 見える — if we make it bright / I'll see it ending

The whole album is people negotiating with light. This is the first negotiation, and she wins it — for one more track.
