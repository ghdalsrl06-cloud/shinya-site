A third SHINYA line begins here. Not the Original albums, not CLUB — **AFTER**, for the hour after the club empties. And it opens with the sound gone.

## The scene it came from

Something was playing a moment ago and now it isn't. Only the edge of the window is blue. The narrator refuses to check the clock, and the reason is exact: 「시계는 안 본다 / 보면 끝난다」.

There is something in this album that the previous seven never had — **two people in the room.** Albums 1 through 5 were nights spent awake alone; album 5 only passed through someone else's room. CLUB danced alone. Here, for the first time, somebody is beside her, and the whole record is about not moving while it ends.

She stays quiet on purpose and then reframes the silence: 「소리를 내면 / 아마 울 거야 / 그래서 가만있어 / 잘도 가만있어」, and decides 「이것도 다정함이라고 / 생각하기로 했다」.

## Sound notes

One of only two tracks on the album with **no drums at all** (the other is track 7). A single Rhodes chord underneath, sub-bass only in the chorus, breath and room tone left in the take. The vocal starts closer to speech than singing.

## One line

> 朝が 来なければ / このままで いられた — if morning never came / we could have stayed like this

And immediately after: 「그런 걸 생각하는 건 / 아마 나뿐」. The album's premise, laid down in its first three minutes.
