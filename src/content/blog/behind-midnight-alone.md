If the 1st album was the night a relationship ends, the 2nd begins on the night after it has ended. Buying two convenience-store bags out of habit and watching one go unused — this is the song where absence gets discovered for the first time.

## The scene it came from

A room unchanged except that the sound is gone. Someone waiting, entrance light still on, for footsteps that aren't coming. The first night you realize you're not bad at being alone — you were just good at being next to that person. That's midnight, where this album starts.

## Sound notes

92 BPM, F-sharp minor. The most minimal track on the record. It opens with room noise — the sound of empty air — and uses almost no drums. A song that draws absence needed its sound to be empty too.

## One line

> 午前0時 ひとりの夜は こんなに長いと 知らなかった — I never knew a night alone at midnight was this long

That sense of length is the whole album's timeline. From midnight to the first train — the record walks this one night in real time.
