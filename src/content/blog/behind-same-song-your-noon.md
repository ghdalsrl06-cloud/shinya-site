They are listening to the same song at the same moment, and it is not the same song.

## The scene it came from

「私は 明け方で / そっちは 正午で / 同じ 曲が / 違う曲に なる」. Heard at night it is a song about endings; heard at noon, 「たぶん 違う」.

Then it turns on the person who made it: 「作った人は / どっちを 思って / 作ったんだろう / たぶん 夜だ」. And the maker doesn't get a say — 「でも 昼に 聴く人を / 止められない / 歌は 選べない / 聴く時間を」.

**This is a song about what this channel does.** Music written at four in the morning ends up in somebody's afternoon, and the ending accepts it: 「私の夜が / 誰かの昼に / 届いてるなら / それで いい」.

## Sound notes

**One melody stated twice in two timbres** — once on a warm Rhodes, once on a thin bright keyboard — over an unchanged groove. The arrangement does exactly what the lyric says: the same song becomes a different song.

## One line

> 歌は 選べない / 聴く時間を — a song doesn’t get to pick / the hour it is heard

The most self-aware track SHINYA has released. It is about the listener you cannot see and cannot schedule.
