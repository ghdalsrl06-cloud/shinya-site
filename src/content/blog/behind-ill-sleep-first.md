Eleven albums in, she goes first.

## The scene it came from

Even the greeting is wrong for the hour: 「「おやすみ」って 言うと / 「いってらっしゃい」みたいだ」.

Then 「先に 寝るね / 初めて 言う」. **On the previous record something was always ahead of her** — the phone battery ran out first (「私より 先に / これが 終わる」), the sky woke first (「空が 先に起きる」). This track calls both of them up and turns them over: 「充電が 先に 切れた / 空が 先に 起きた / 今日は 私が / 先に 終わる」.

It does not close: 「そっちの昼は 続く / 続いてて いい / 私は ここで 終わる」. And because somebody is always awake in shifts, 「だから この夜は / 終わらない」.

The last gesture answers the last album exactly. That one ended with 「カーテンを / 開けてしまった」 — a curtain opened. Here it is **closed**: 「カーテンを 閉める / 今度は 閉める方だ / 明るくなる前に / 先に 寝る」.

## Sound notes

**Back to the opening track’s setup** — drums gone entirely, Rhodes and sub-bass only, the harmony thinning until one voice is left. And **the reverb opens instead of closing on the last line.**

## One line

> カーテンを 閉める / 今度は 閉める方だ — I close the curtain / this time I’m the one closing it

The AFTER line has always closed things — a light switched off, a room emptied. This one closes the curtain and leaves the sound open, because what ends here is only her. The afternoon on the other side carries on, and there is no closing that.
