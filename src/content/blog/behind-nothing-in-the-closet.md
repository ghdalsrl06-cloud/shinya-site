A habit outlives the thing it was for.

## The scene it came from

「クローゼットを 開ける / 何も ない / 分かってるのに / また 開ける」. Nothing has changed since the last time she checked, and she checks anyway.

One hanger was missed on the way out and swings on its own. Everything else is a single rod lying across an empty space: 「入れるものが ない / 出すものも ない / **開ける理由が / もう ない**」.

A winter coat used to hang here and stayed up through the summer. **The coat that ran through tracks three and five of the previous album is already gone from this one** — which is how you know how much time has passed between the two records.

The bridge asks a question and refuses to answer it: 「空っぽを 見て / 安心するのは / なんでだろう / 分からないまま」.

## Sound notes

**The first track where the 808 sub enters**, taking over from the silence of track one. A short attack like a door catching becomes the rhythm, and a muted guitar answers into the empty space.

## One line

> 空っぽを 見て / 安心するのは / なんでだろう / 分からないまま — why does looking at empty / put me at ease / I wonder / and never find out

She does not resolve it, and the album never comes back to it. Some relief has no explanation attached.
