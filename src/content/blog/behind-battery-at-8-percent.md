An object gets tired before the person does.

## The scene it came from

Eight percent. The charger is within reach and she doesn't move: 「手を 伸ばせば 届く / 届くのに / 起き上がらない」. **Not can't — won't.**

Then the coldest line here: 「私より 先に / これが 終わる / 機械のほうが / 正直だ」. The battery knows how to end. She doesn't.

She can't even remember what she was looking at — 「何を 見てたか / 思い出せない / 指だけが 動いてた」. The phone dies, and it changes nothing: 「切れた / 暗くなった / それでも / まだ 起きてる」.

## Sound notes

**The driest mix on the album.** The reverb is pulled back, the tap of a finger sits close, and one electric-piano figure repeats in the same place throughout. D minor.

## One line

> 私より 先に / これが 終わる / 機械のほうが / 正直だ — before I do / this will end / the machine / is the honest one

「私より 先に」 appears twice on this album. Here it is a phone. In track five it is the sky.
