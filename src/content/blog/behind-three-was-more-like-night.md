This is the centre of the record, and the one place the album title is explained head-on.

## The scene it came from

「三時なら / まだ 起きてるだけ / 四時になると / 寝てないになる」 — at three you are still up; at four you haven't slept. The dark is identical, the sky the same colour, and only the word changes: 「同じ暗さなのに / 呼び方が 変わる / それだけで 疲れる」. **Track one said the hour has no name; this is the answer.**

Then the album calls the previous record by name: 「いちばん長いのは / 三時から 四時 / 誰かが 言ってた / 本当だった」. The someone is **the 5th album, track three** — 「三時から四時が いちばん長い」 is in it. *The previous album named that hour the longest one and never went there. This album is standing in it.*

What she wants back isn't the time but the word: 「名前だけでも / 夜のままで」.

## Sound notes

**The slowest and widest track**, at 68 BPM. The chords are voiced far apart over a long drone, and the percussion thins to a soft brush. B minor — the only track here in B.

## One line

> 同じ暗さなのに / 呼び方が 変わる / それだけで 疲れる — the same dark, and still / what you call it changes / and that alone is tiring

Nothing happens in this album, and this is why: the name changing is the event.
