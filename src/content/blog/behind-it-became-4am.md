The album opens on a glance at a phone.

## The scene it came from

Four o'clock exactly. The first four lines set the posture the whole record keeps: 「見なきゃ よかった / 知らなければ / まだ 三時だった / そういうことに できた」 — she could have gone on calling it three.

Nothing happened. 「数字が 変わっただけ / なのに 違う」. And the hour has no word for itself: 「今は 何なんだろう / 名前が ない」. **That one line is what track four comes back to answer.**

Even the reason is not what you would expect: 「眠るのが 怖い / わけじゃない / ただ 今日が / 終わってない」. Not fear. Just an unfinished day.

## Sound notes

**The emptiest track on the album.** One synth pad and a short tick like a second hand; the bass only enters on the chorus. A minor. The closer returns to exactly this.

## One line

> 今は 何なんだろう / 名前が ない — what is this now / it has no name

Remember the missing name. Track four is about nothing but that.
