The verdict, and it is not the one she had prepared.

## The scene it came from

She looks in the mirror on purpose this time — track one avoided it — and the haircut is fine: 「短くなった 髪 / 思ったより 似合ってる」. No reply yet, and 「それでも いい」.

Then the line the whole record was built to reach: 「後悔するって 決めてたのに / してない / 全然 してない」. **She had scheduled the regret and it did not turn up.**

The chorus finally says the album's title kindly: 「明日の私が なんとかする / 今夜の私は 今夜のぶん」. Track one called her a stranger, track two made her apologise, track four was frightened of her. Here she is simply trusted.

And it does not pretend to be a cure. 「昼の私は また 飲み込む / それで いい / 夜が 来れば いい」 — daytime will go back to swallowing it, and that is fine, because night comes round again.

Then the song looks outside itself for the first time: 「やらかしてる 人が / きっと もう ひとり いる / その人にも 言いたい / 今夜も やっちゃえ って」. **That is the door into the hidden track.**

The last four lines return to the album's very first: 「言いたいことを 飲み込む癖 / 明日も たぶん そう / それでも 夜が 来れば / また 手が 動く」.

## Sound notes

**Track one's figure comes back in a major key.** The swing is the widest and brightest here, the hook is stacked three deep, and the final chorus lifts a semitone. It is the longest track on the album, and it needs the room.

## One line

> 明日の私が なんとかする — tomorrow's me will handle it somehow

The same four words that opened the record as a complaint close it as a plan.
