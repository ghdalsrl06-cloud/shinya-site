Three excuses prepared. The steps for fading out memorized, and the method for vanishing clean. This is the song of someone who has always called ending-things-before-they-hurt "strength." The album's climax — and the first time the emotion actually raises its voice.

## The scene it came from

Packing more the deeper you fall. Looking at a room only as something you'll leave. Editing a goodbye in the drafts folder over and over, never sending it. The heart of the track is the confession that they never even counted how many times they lost someone this way.

## Sound notes

94 BPM, C minor. The bass runs overdriven — the biggest, roughest sound on the record. And in the final chorus, for the only time on the album, the voice breaks open. Someone who always disappeared quietly says a thing out loud for the first time: "and still — I'm still here."

## One line

> 逃げるのは 楽だった 残るほうが ずっと 怖い — Running was the easy part; staying is far more frightening

Staying, with your escape fully prepared. That's the entire definition of courage this album has.
