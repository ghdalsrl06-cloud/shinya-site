Eleven records in, this one opens the two doors the channel had kept shut.

## The scene it came from

Across 64 tracks the exclude list has said the same two things every time: no `bright cheerful pop`, no `chiptune`. This song is both. A busy piano riff underneath, an 8-bit lead answering the vocal in the chorus, 136 BPM, and the last chorus a note above the others.

It was written the way YOASOBI writes: **the story first, and the lyrics are its summary.** The story is one page. The person in the daytime is nobody. At ten at night they stand at the mirror, erase today's face into a drawer, and take out the name kept there for the night. There is no venue. The stage is nowhere and everywhere: the city's windows coming on one by one are the seats, and the screens held up in the dark by people who cannot sleep are the glow sticks. In the front row there is a seat always left open, and it is 君's.

The whole song has exactly one event, and it is 「夜の名前を 取り出す」 — picking up the white cap off the drawer. The cap that reads `SHINYA` is this channel's name, so putting it on **is** walking onto the stage.

「開演」 means the curtain going up. The Korean keeps 「막이 오른다」 rather than the literal reading, so it pairs with the bridge's 「幕が下りる / 막이 내린다」.

## Sound notes

「今夜も 開演」 lands seven times, 「夜にしか」 six, 「最前列」 three. Verse 2 is four lines packed 1.8 seconds apart and is over in **seven seconds** — the daytime is short, and 「誰にも気づかれない 交差点」 is all of it.

After the bridge's 「拍手は 聞こえないけど / 君が眠れた なら / それが アンコール」 there is a **16.5-second build with no words at all.** The final chorus starts 7 seconds down at -18dB and the full band arrives at 2:07. Then 15.7 seconds of outro after 「明日も 開演」.

## The video

The channel's first full music video: 2:40, twenty segments, fixed master frames rather than frame chaining.

Its axis is not day against night — the lyrics already do that. It is **the order in which you find out where the stage is.** Room, then the window opposite lighting up, then the roof where the city lights a window on every beat. The daytime crossing is at 30% saturation with faceless silhouettes. In chorus two the seats run to the horizon and one empty window — 君's — lights up. Through the bridge the windows go out one at a time, 君's among them, and at 2:07 the whole city comes back on at once. That is the encore.

**One rule holds the whole thing together: a lit window is an audience, a dark one is someone asleep.** There is no lip sync — 深夜 was shot as someone standing on a stage, not someone singing on one.

## One line

> 拍手は 聞こえないけど / 君が眠れた なら / それが アンコール — I can't hear any applause / but if you managed to sleep / that's the encore

The video ends on its first frame, with the cap back on the drawer. Eleven albums have been about the night not ending well. This one ends with 「明日も 開演」 — it opens again tomorrow.
