The album opens on the night before it happens.

## The scene it came from

The first line is a habit, not an event: 「言いたいことを 飲み込む癖 / 何年も うまくなった」. **Years of practice at saying nothing.** She drinks one mouthful of water at the fridge and the only thing in the room is 「喉が鳴る音だけ / やけに 大きい」 — a swallow, absurdly loud, because nothing else is happening.

The evidence piles up quietly. Three days of an untouched cart, hair left to grow, and a mirror she avoids for a reason she states plainly: 「見たら 何か 始まりそうで」. Then the album's title arrives for the first time, as an accusation: 「全部 明日 明日って 言って / 明日の私は いつも 他人だ」. **Tomorrow's me is a stranger** — someone else's problem, filed away.

And holding back earns nothing: 「我慢は 静かで どこにも 残らない」.

Then the bridge changes temperature in one line. 「それでも 今夜は 指が 熱い」, and 「はさみは まだ 引き出しの中 / でも もう 手が 動いてる」. The scissors have not moved. The hand has.

## Sound notes

**The most restrained track here.** The low end is held down and the hi-hats run ahead of it; the bass grips one note and refuses to move. **There is no pre-chorus before the first chorus** — someone holding back does not build up to it. And it carries the album's only post-chorus, pressing 「Hold it」 once more after the chorus has already finished, the way a swallowed sentence comes back.

## One line

> 明日の私は いつも 他人だ — tomorrow's me is always a stranger

Four tracks later she will be frightened of that stranger, and by track seven she will be handing things over to her on purpose. It starts here, as a complaint.
