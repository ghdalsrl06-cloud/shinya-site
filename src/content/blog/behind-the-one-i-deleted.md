The photos, the chats, the name — all deleted. It was their own thumb that pressed "yes" on "Delete this?" But dialing the number, the fingers know it halfway through. They never forgot the rhythm.

## The scene it came from

The feeling that something is still glowing on a screen wiped clean. Only the screen got erased — and some things, like correction-pen marks, only get darker the more you erase. The failure of deletion is the whole subject.

## Sound notes

94 BPM, B minor. Glitch and reverse form the skeleton. The vocal is chopped into stutters that cut off mid-word, as if being deleted — the sound of the delete button, performed by a voice. And where it cuts, a reversed tail lingers, the way afterimages do.

## One line

> 消したのは 画面だけだった — All I deleted was the screen

The entire track exists for this one line. The deletion completed successfully, and nothing was deleted.
