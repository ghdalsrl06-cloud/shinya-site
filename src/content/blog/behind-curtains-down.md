The record opens up exactly once, and it happens here.

## The scene it came from

Twelve hooks, counted as they come off. 「遮るものが / なくなった / 向かいの 窓が / はっきり 見える」.

The reason they stayed closed is given plainly: 「昼も 閉めてた / 暗いほうが / 落ち着いたから」. Shut in daylight too, because darker was easier.

And then, with the curtains off: 「今 見ると / ただの 窓だ / 怖くも 何とも / ない」. Whatever the window meant, it does not mean it any more.

The bridge pairs with the opening track and reverses it: 「二年 いたのに / 知らなかった / **朝の この部屋を** / 知らなかった」. In track one it was 「それでも 二年 / ここに いた」 — I was here. Here it is: I never knew it. **Two years, claimed once and disowned once.**

## Sound notes

**The only major key on the album.** The harmony finally opens, the Rhodes widens, and the 808 turns warm and round. Even the reverb goes suddenly bright. The other six tracks are all minor.

## One line

> 二年 いたのに / 知らなかった / 朝の この部屋を / 知らなかった — two years here / and I never knew / this room in the morning / I never knew it

The brightest track on a record about leaving is the one where she finally looks out of the window. Then the next song turns the light off.
