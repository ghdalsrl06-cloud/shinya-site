The record stops describing a night and starts talking to you.

## The scene it came from

The first line is an observation about who is listening: 「七曲 ぜんぶ 聴いたんだね / こんな時間まで」, and then the diagnosis — 「何かを 決めかねてる人だけが / ここまで 来る」. **Only someone who cannot decide something gets this far.**

She reports her own night as a completed thing: 「私は 全部 やった / 髪も 自分で 切った / 送っても しまった」, and 「朝には 全部 バレる / それでも 後悔してない」.

Then she describes you, accurately and without triumph: the draft is still unsent, the scissors are untouched, nothing has changed — 「でも さっきから / 同じ画面を 見てるでしょう / 指が 少し 動いた / やめようと してる」. And immediately she takes the sting out of it: 「知ってる その感じ / 私も 三時間 そうだった」.

The best thing about this song is that it does not pretend to be generous. It states its own motive: 「一人で やると 怖いから」, 「共犯が ほしいだけ」, and then, without being asked, 「ずるい こと 言ってる」. **She knows it is a cheap thing to say and says it anyway.**

So it does not push: 「言わないよ どっちが いいとか / 朝に 困るのは 君だし」. The last thing it does is hand over the choice — 「決めるのは 君だ / 髪でも 下書きでも いい / 今夜のうちに ひとつ」.

## Sound notes

**The emptiest track on the album.** One Rhodes chord, brush snaps, and no bass at all until the final section — the voice runs almost alone. It is a song that speaks to someone, so the arrangement steps out of the way.

## One line

> 決めるのは 君だ — it's yours to decide

Three AFTER records took an ending that arrived. This one makes one, and then, for the first time, passes the turn across.
