This is the maths you do at four in the morning.

## The scene it came from

Sleep now and you wake at midday; wake at midday and the evening is already late; think it's late and you do nothing; think you can do nothing and **it is night again**. The sum closes into a circle.

「計算だけが / 早くなる / 早くなっても / 答えは 同じ」 — that is why this is the fastest track here.

Staying awake leaves the day intact but not usable: 「残っても / 使えるわけじゃない / 分かってるのに / 指で 数えてる」. Six hours, five hours, counted on fingers.

The song refuses to conclude: 「どっちも 選べない / 選ばないうちに / 四時が 過ぎる」. **Nothing is chosen, the hour passes anyway, and that is exactly why there has to be a seventh track.**

## Sound notes

**The fastest and busiest track**: 96 BPM, tight sixteenth hi-hats, a bass that cuts off short. The arrangement is packed, as though it were counting. G minor.

## One line

> どっちも 選べない / 選ばないうちに / 四時が 過ぎる — I can’t choose either one / and while I don’t choose / four o’clock goes past

A track that ends without deciding is not a flaw here. It is the setup.
