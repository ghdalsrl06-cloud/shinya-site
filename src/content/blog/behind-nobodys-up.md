The peak of the album, and it happens with the lights off and the volume down.

## The scene it came from

An empty room, sound turned low, and the body moving on its own anyway. The only audience is his own reflection in the window glass. Being bad at it is fine, because 「아무도 안 본다」.

The condition attached is the sharpest idea on the record: 「잘하려고 하면 / 재미없어진다 / 아무도 없으니까 / 가장 자유롭다」. Freedom here isn't the absence of rules, it's the absence of witnesses. He tiptoes so the floor won't carry it downstairs, apologizes in advance — 「아랫집 사람이 / 깼다면 미안」 — and then refuses to stop: 「지금 이 삼 분만 / 아무한테도 안 준다」.

## Sound notes

The widest mix on the album. A long reverb in an empty hall lets the chopped vocal hook answer itself, and the keys are reduced to stabs. E major.

## One line

> 誰も いないから いちばん 自由だ — because nobody is here, I'm the most free

Every dance track is written for a room full of people. This one is written for an empty one.
