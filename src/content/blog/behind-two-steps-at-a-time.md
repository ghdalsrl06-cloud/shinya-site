Track three is where effort starts to feel good. The body isn't just answering now; it's asking for more.

## The scene it came from

Skipping a step, then another. On the landing he looks back once — nobody is chasing him — and keeps climbing anyway. From the seventh-floor window the town shrinks and only convenience store lights stay strung out below. His calves are burning and he knows exactly what tomorrow will feel like.

The turn is quiet and it changes the record: 「숨이 차는 게 / 기분 좋다니 / 지금까지 몰랐다」. He also admits he hasn't thought about coming back down — 「올라가는 동안만은 아무것도 무섭지 않다」. Climbing works only as long as he doesn't look at the descent.

## Sound notes

The synth arpeggio climbs one step per bar — the title lives inside the arrangement. Hi-hats chopped and doubled, sub-bass short and dry. G major, the brightest key on the record so far.

## One line

> 下は 見ない — never look down

Two words of technique, and the whole strategy of the night.
