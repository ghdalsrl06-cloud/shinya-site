This is the song of the person who laughed their way past a mention of last summer. Who listened to a song they used to love, pretending not to know it, the night the other person played it. Staying silent while knowing every secret pushes them further away.

## The scene it came from

"I should have just said it took me back — I could have told you everything from there." The song keeps circling that one missed moment. At its center is the image of deleting old photos and then finding your hand frozen — together with the question: could you love me whole, past and all?

## Sound notes

82 BPM, B minor. The slowest track on the record — and the silences are deliberately wide. It's a song about a story that couldn't be told, so the spaces without sound had to become part of the lyrics.

## One line

> 過去を持ってる私ごと 好きになって もらえるのかな — Could you love me whole, past and all that comes with it

The question never gets an answer inside this song. The answer is postponed all the way to track 7.
