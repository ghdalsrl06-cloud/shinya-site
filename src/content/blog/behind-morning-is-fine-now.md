The album ends by giving up the thing it spent six tracks defending.

## The scene it came from

「決めた わけじゃない / 諦めた わけでもない / ただ カーテンを / 開けてしまった」 — **the decision is made by the body.** The light is in and there is no putting it back, so 「それで いい」.

Track two turns over here: 「寝るのを やめる / 負けでも いい / 今日は そういう日 / それだけのこと」. The person who couldn't sleep because 「자면 지는 것 같아서」 now stays up **saying losing is fine**. The same action, the opposite reason.

The chorus is the album's sentence: 「もう 朝でいい / 夜が 終わった / もう 朝でいい / **私は 終わってない**」.

She makes coffee and performs the morning: 「体は 覚えている」. And the hour in the title is filed away as nothing: 「四時は 過ぎた / 過ぎてしまえば / 何でも なかった / 毎日 そうだ」. **Then the last line is 「明日も たぶん 四時」.**

## Sound notes

**Back to the opening track’s emptiness** — one pad and the bass. But on the last line **the reverb opens instead of closing.** A minor.

## One line

> もう 朝でいい / 夜が 終わった / もう 朝でいい / 私は 終わってない — morning is fine now / the night is over / morning is fine now / I’m not over

The previous album closed by turning a light off and cutting the reverb dead. This one opens a curtain and lets it ring. **AFTER ends by closing; Original ends by opening** — and 「明日も たぶん 四時」 means it does not close at all.
