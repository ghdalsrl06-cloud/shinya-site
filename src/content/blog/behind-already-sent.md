This is the track where the night stops being reversible.

## The scene it came from

It starts as care: 「三行 書いて 二行 消して / また 書いて また 消して」. Writing and deleting is still safe. Then the finger decides without her — 「ほんの 一回 / 触れただけ / それで 全部 いった」.

The regret is measured precisely. 「既読が つく前なら / まだ 間に合ったのに」, except 「指は もう 離れてた / 一秒が 足りなかった」. **One second.** That is the whole margin the song gives her.

Then the waiting, which is worse than the sending: a minute is long, three are longer, 「点が 三つ 出たり 消えたり」, and the phone gets turned face-down and picked up again. And in the middle of it, the line that makes this an album about doing things rather than an album about regret: 「怖いのに / なんで 笑ってるんだろう」.

The chorus makes its peace by giving up control, not by winning: 「戻す ボタンは どこにも ない / だから もう 考えなくて いい / 返事は 来なくても いい」.

## Sound notes

**A send tone opens the chorus** — the actual sound of the thing that cannot be undone. This carries the heaviest kick on the record, with a short, dry sub-bass under it and almost nothing else. The moment of going through with it is the moment the arrangement is hardest.

## One line

> 世界が 少し 動いた — the world moved a little

Not *I ruined it*. Something that was inside is now outside, and the size of it is honest: a little.
