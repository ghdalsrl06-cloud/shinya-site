Nothing is decided in this song. It is done first, and looked at afterwards.

## The scene it came from

The scissors are on the table and she does not remember putting them there: 「なんで ここに 置いたんだろう」. The answer never comes, because the next line has already overtaken the question — 「考える前に もう 持ってた」.

The act itself is three short lines with a held breath in the middle: 「一房 つまんで 息を 止めて / そのまま 切った」. The mirror is on her side: 「鏡の中の 私が 笑ってる / やっちゃえ って 言ってる」. And the first thing she notices is not the look but the weight — 「短くなった 分だけ 軽く なった」.

Then the song names what is actually happening. 「頭は まだ 止めようとしてる / 手は もう 聞いてない」 — **the head is still arguing and the hand has stopped listening.** Which leads straight to the deal the whole album runs on: 「謝るのは 朝の私 / 今の私は 今しか いない」.

## Sound notes

**This is the sound the hands make.** Dry, short percussion — a drawer, the scissors, finger snaps — lands slightly *ahead* of the beat, while the kick arrives late in the bar. The body is early and the pulse is catching up.

And it is **the only track with no pre-chorus at all**: the verse drops straight into the chorus. A run-up would give her somewhere to think.

## One line

> 謝るのは 朝の私 / 今の私は 今しか いない — morning-me does the apologising / this me only exists right now

Track one called tomorrow's self a stranger. This one puts her to work.
