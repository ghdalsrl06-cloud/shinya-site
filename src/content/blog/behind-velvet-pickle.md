A new line starts here. SHINYA CLUB is what happens when the 3AM character leaves the dark room, walks into a club, and decides — just for one record — that luck is allowed. And the first thing worth singing about? A jar of pickles glowing in the back of the fridge like a lucky charm.

## The scene it came from

Opening the fridge at night for no reason, and something ordinary suddenly looks precious: green glass next to the mustard, catching the light. The song treats that dumb little crush completely seriously — one bite, and the tiny kitchen turns into a stage. The CLUB line's whole thesis is in that move: worthless things, maximum devotion.

## Sound notes

128 BPM, the classic Jersey club five-kick bounce with a bed-squeak sample riding the triplets. The word "pickle" gets chopped and stuttered like a drum — the hook is basically percussion you can sing. Where the main line albums pull everything toward silence, this one pulls everything toward the bounce.

## One line

> 甘くて とがってる — sweet and sharp at once

That's the pickle, and that's the whole new line: sweetness with an edge, played at club volume.
