Morning arrives without being asked for, and this time it is not a threat.

## The scene it came from

She did not sleep and her eyes are wide open anyway: 「部屋が 白くなってる / いつのまにか 朝だ」. Then she sweeps the hair off the floor and the song quietly rhymes with its own opening — 「音が やけに 大きい」, the exact phrase track one used for the sound of swallowing water. **The same room, the same absurd loudness, on the other side of the night.**

The evidence is sorted in one line: 「下書きは 消えてる / 送ったものは 送ったまま」. Drafts gone, what was sent stays sent.

Light gets the album's most unsentimental description: 「光は 正直で / 昨日を そのまま 照らす」 — **honest, and therefore not on her side.** She opens the curtain anyway, and the reason is not courage: 「隠すものが もう ない」. There is simply nothing left to hide.

The ending is deliberately small. She might get told off, she might get laughed at, 「どっちでも いい」, and then: 「とりあえず 顔を 洗う / 今日の ぶんだけ やる / それで じゅうぶん」.

## Sound notes

**The first major key on the album** (E♭). The space widens, the highs come in, and the harmony stacks upward instead of sitting still. The hook is 「Let it in」 — the sound of a curtain opening rather than a resolution being reached.

## One line

> 今日の ぶんだけ やる / それで じゅうぶん — I do today's share and no more / that is enough

An album about going too far ends its morning track by doing the smallest possible amount.
