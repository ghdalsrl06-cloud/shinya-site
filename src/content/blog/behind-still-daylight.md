The album opens on a reply that arrives at the wrong hour.

## The scene it came from

She asks 「そっちは 何時」 and then admits she doesn't need to: 「聞かなくても 分かる」. The shadows in the corner of the photo are short, so it is daytime there. **Knowing a time difference and seeing it are not the same thing** — 「昼だ / ちゃんと 明るい」.

Then the sentence the whole record works on: 「私は 終わる / そっちは 続く / 同じ日なのに / 違う場所にいる」. **Only one of them is ending.** Seven tracks take that one line apart.

And it closes not on comfort but on a fact: 「起きてる人が / どこかに いる / それだけで / 少し 楽になる」.

## Sound notes

**The emptiest track on the album.** Rhodes and sub-bass, almost no percussion, and **a notification tone standing in for the rhythm** — the sound the reply made. The vocal starts closer to speech, the way you read a message. The closer returns here.

## One line

> 私は 終わる / そっちは 続く / 同じ日なのに / 違う場所にいる — I end / you keep going / the same day / in a different place

Nothing happens on this record. The same fact is touched seven times from seven angles, and this is the first.
